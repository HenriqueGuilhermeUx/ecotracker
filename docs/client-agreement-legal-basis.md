# Legal basis notes

Operational references reviewed for the Client Agreement Gate:

- MP 2.200-2/2001, art. 10: electronic documents and other means of proving authorship/integrity when accepted by the parties.
- LGPD, art. 7, V: personal-data processing when necessary to perform a contract or related preliminary procedures.
- CPC, art. 784, §4, added by Law 14.620/2023: electronic titles may use legally recognized electronic signatures; witnesses are dispensable when integrity is verified by a signature provider.

The EcoTracker built-in acceptance is an audit trail and not represented as ICP-Brasil or qualified signature. Before first live high-value transaction, the contract template and signature level should be reviewed by Brazilian counsel and a signature provider may be integrated.
